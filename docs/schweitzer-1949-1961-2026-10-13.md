## Method
- Every statement was **transcribed afresh from the page images** (150 dpi pages; every formula, exponent, index and interval re-read at 300 dpi). The older staged drafts in `docs/schweitzer/` (AoPS-based or paraphrased "restatements") were **not** reused.
- The text is the book's own English wording, with LaTeX. Section marks such as "(R. 15)" are kept as `book_section`; they set the topic (A → Algebra, N → Number Theory, G → Geometry, C → Combinatorics, F/R/S → Analysis; probability and set-theory problems and the four unmarked ones by content).
- Footnotes omitted: 1949 P3 (Pólya–Szegő reference) and 1955 P6 (Erdős, *Amer. Math. Monthly* 53).
- Year totals agree with the book's Chapter 2 table (PDF p. 37): 1949 8; 1950/I 10; 1950/II 9; 1951 17; 1952–1957 10 each; 1958 11; 1959–1961 10 each.
- QA: all 145 rendered with MathJax 3 (866 formulas, no errors) and the difficult ones inspected visually; every prose word cross-checked against the book's OCR layer (only OCR garbling found); browser check of the row, the two 1950 rounds and the provenance badges.
