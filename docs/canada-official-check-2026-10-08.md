# Canada CMO: check of 1971–2010 against the official CMS papers (8 Oct 2026)

**Source.** Official exam papers from the Canadian Mathematical Society: `https://cms.math.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/examYYYY.pdf`.

**Already checked earlier.** 1969, 1970 and 2011–2026 had source checks before this work.

## Method
- Each paper was split into problems.
- Each of the 223 statements was compared with the paper by words and by digits.
- Every mismatch was checked by hand.
- Most mismatches were PDF artefacts: lost "fi/ff" ligatures, "6" for ∠, and page footers.
- Problems that refer to a diagram were checked on the page images.

## Result
- No mathematical errors were found.
- The text already followed the official wording.
- Official figures were missing in 9 problems. They were added, cropped from the paper:

| Problem | Change |
|---|---|
| 1971 P1 | Added "(See diagram.)" and `images/canada_1971_p1_fig.png` |
| 1977 P2 | Added `images/canada_1977_p2_fig.png` |
| 1977 P7 | Added "(see diagram)" and `images/canada_1977_p7_fig.png` |
| 1982 P1 | Official wording "In the diagram, …" (the stored text had paraphrased the setting) and `images/canada_1982_p1_fig.png` |
| 1992 P3 | Official wording "In the diagram, …"; P and Q are defined only by the figure. Added `images/canada_1992_p3_fig.png` |
| 2001 P2 | Official wording "There is a board numbered −10 to 10 as shown" and `images/canada_2001_p2_fig.png` |
| 2001 P3 | Added `images/canada_2001_p3_fig.png` |
| 2004 P2 | Official wording "the 9 × 9 chessboard (shown here)"; the figure fixes the colouring. Added `images/canada_2004_p2_fig.png` |
| 2010 P1 | Added the 5-staircase and the tilings for f(2), f(4): `images/canada_2010_p1_fig1.png` and `…_fig2.png` |

All 323 Canada statements now have source checks.
