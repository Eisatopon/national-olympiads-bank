## Explicit source correction

2023 P1 prints `f: N × N → N`, with `f(1,1)=2` and `f(a,b+1)=f(a,b)-b`. These conditions give `f(1,3)=-1`, contradicting the stated codomain. The bank uses the integer codomain Z and positive-integer inputs. This correction is displayed to readers and carries **no exact-source badge**. The other 24 statements have AI-assisted source-fidelity checks. This is source comparison, not a claim of independent solution certification.
