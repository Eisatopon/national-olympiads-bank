# Czech-Slovak MO 1952–1999: checked against the official annual reports (14 Oct 2026)

All 230 stored final-round (III. kolo, category A) statements of 1952–1999 that came from AoPS (every year except 1959, 1967, 1968, 1970, 1980, which were added/checked earlier the same day) were compared, page image by page image, with the official Czechoslovak MO annual reports:
- 1952–1994 and 1996: "N. ročník matematické olympiády. Zpráva o řešení úloh ze soutěže …" (Státní pedagogické nakladatelství), digitised by DML-CZ (dml.cz/handle/10338.dmlcz/404411); the exact chapter of each year is in the per-problem ledger.
- 1995, 1997, 1998, 1999: Slovak MO annual reports "Správa o riešení úloh" (skmo.sk/dokument.php?id=2173, 2171, 2170, 2169).

Result: 174 stored translations were faithful and kept; 56 were replaced by faithful translations of the official text. Every year has the full paper (4 problems 1952–1966, 6 problems from 1969); nothing was missing or in the wrong year.

Real mathematical errors fixed (selection): 1954 P2 (isosceles right triangle), 1961 P3 (cyclists start from the same place), 1961 P4 (wrong question), 1962 P3 (perpendicular skew lines, answer in r and v), 1969 P3 (positive integer terms), 1971 P1 (the source assumes the numbers are not all zero, not xyz ≠ 0), 1973 P5 (rotation of Q about P, not P about Q), 1975 P1 (acute triangle), 1978 P3 (missing part a), 1984 P2 (“or a parallelogram”), 1986 P6 (“distinct”), 1990 P5 (roads cross only in towns), 1991 P6 (union, not partition), 1994 P3 (no closed polygon assumed), 1995 P5 (internal/external tangency), 1997 P3 (convex polyhedra meeting pairwise), 1997 P4 (missing second question), 1998 P1 (positive reals). Added paraphrase-only restorations (requests for discussion/construction, original dimensions, source notation).

Notes: editorial corrections of two source misprints without check badge (1957 P4 diagonals AC, BD; 1961 P4 vertex Y on AD); source ambiguity 1972 P5 (“pairs”, unordered per the official solution); other source remarks recorded as notes. New statement figures: cs_1986_p3_fig.png (seven-cube piece), cs_1985_p5_fig.png (triangular table). Days from 1969: the reports rarely state which problem was on which day; P1–3 / P4–6 by printed order.
