Absent labels are **gaps in these collections**, not proof of the dates, composition, senior category or selection status of absent exams. The highest displayed label is not proof that there were no later tests. School-year headings include tests from the preceding calendar year; do not silently relabel them.

All seven collections were read as extracted PDF text to inventory headings and numbered entries. Only 2025 PDF page 1 and 2026 PDF page 3 were additionally inspected as rendered images in this audit. No claim that all 19 PDF pages were visually source-checked. Those two images confirm the two-entry first test of 2025 as printed and the indexing issue in the final 2026 entry. No statements or solutions were copied into the bank.
