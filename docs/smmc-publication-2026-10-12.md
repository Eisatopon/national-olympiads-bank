# Simon Marais publication review

The Undergraduate year grid now includes all 88 problem statements from the 22 question papers linked in the organiser's 2017–2025 archive. The archive was checked on 12 October 2026. It did not list a 2026 question paper at that time, so no 2026 edition or question has been inferred.

The collection is `undergraduate/Simon-Marais-problems.json`. All statements use English text and LaTeX. No solutions or full-statement facsimiles are included. Every statement was compared with the rendered problem page of its official PDF. Source URLs, document SHA-256 hashes, statement hashes, page numbers and paper identities are retained. The independent statement snapshots in `metadata/smmc-statement-checks.json` drive the existing source-check badges and invalidate them if a statement changes.

| Editions | Official papers | Problems per year |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2017–2020 | Session A, Session B | 8 |
| 2021 | Session A, Session B; B was the only West paper | 8 |
| 2022–2025 | Paper A, Paper B, Paper C | 12 |

Paper A is the first East session; Paper B is shared between the second East and first West sessions from 2022 onward; Paper C is the second West session. Papers are not labelled as chronological days. Official A1–C4 identities and all subparts are preserved.

The only raster asset is the diagram-only crop for 2023/A1, including both spiral examples and their labels. The 2020/A2 card-stack example is represented with a LaTeX array. The 2022/A3 long equation is broken across two aligned lines without changing its mathematical content.

Particular extraction errors corrected from the rendered source include the speed 1/2018 in 2018/B3, the factor outside the exponential in 2019/B1, the recurrence parentheses in 2021/A2, the 2^n cardinality and 1/e^2 limit in 2022/A2, the three separate entries in the minimum in 2022/C1, the k/2023 threshold in 2023/C4, multiplication after the floor in 2024/A1, and the linear jump length 2i−1 in 2025/C1.

Historical notices that a problem was open, along with the scoring remarks, remain part of the original statement. They do not certify its current research status. Topic categories use the existing four bank categories. Problem DNA technique lists remain empty until a separate mathematical review supports them.

Publication follows the project owner's explicit instruction to complete and publish each undergraduate competition and their request to proceed with Simon Marais on 12 October 2026. The National, Regional, International and Tournament collection files are unchanged.
