# IMC statements — rights and verification provenance

Updated 2026-10-12.

## Publication authorization reported by project owner
The Eisatopon project owner expressly reports having received an **explicit oral assurance that they may publish the IMC mathematical problem statements**. Treat this as the project's reported authorization, and **do not repeatedly ask the owner to obtain the same permission**. The grantor's identity and exact scope have not been independently recorded here; this note is not a legal adjudication. Attribution to the competition and organizers should be retained.

## Separate editorial acceptance criterion
Permission to publish text and reliability of mathematical transcription are independent considerations:
- No unsourced mathematical symbols, guessed images or incorrect hypotheses should be presented as original.
- The IMC research JSON currently carries `publication_eligible=false` as a *content quality/review gate*, not as a claim the project owner lacks publication permission.
- Verified source comparisons may be accepted individually with organizer URL, page, method and date.
- Automated text/PDF lexical overlap can identify candidates but **cannot certify exact notation, missing diagrams, or comprehensive mathematical fidelity**.
- Our database stores competition **statements only**, not official solutions.

## Collection progress
All 33 years 1994–2026 have research draft entries totaling 354 statements. All official IMC year pages for 1999–2026 were accessible in the first automated source acquisition job (28/28); all 28 annual sets had downloadable PDF exam source materials in the subsequent audit. In the PDF text screening, 277/294 statements had substantial lexical overlap, and 17 need high-priority manual inspection. This *screening* does not change the count of visually source-verified statements.
