# Austrian MO (ÖMO) final round, 1970–2026 (15 Oct 2026)

Collection: `Austria-oemo-problems.json` (key `at`). Per-problem record: `docs/austria-final-2026-10-15.json`.
Scope: the final round of the federal competition (Bundeswettbewerb; from 2010 "Federal Competition for Advanced Students, Part 2"), senior/Advanced Level only. Part 1 (2002–2014) and the Beginner Level papers (1989, 1991) are not included. 1970 is a single four-problem paper; every other year has two days of three problems.

## 2015–2026: official check (72 statements)
Every stored statement was compared with the official English paper at `oemo.at/OeMO/problems/BWF/<year>`, with the German paper (`/OeMO/aufgaben/BWF/<year>`) as cross-check. All 72 are the right final-round problems. The stored texts were independent translations with the same mathematics; they were replaced by the official English wording (70), two already matched. Substantive differences: 2021 P3 (integers $a,b,c\ge 0$, not "natural numbers"), 2021 P5 (the official English adds an introductory sentence absent from the German paper; kept), 2020 P1 and P5 (English omits "convex" and "interior", kept as in the English paper). Paper typos normalised: 2015 P5 "und", 2015 P6 "configurations", 2021 P3 "und". 72 source checks.
