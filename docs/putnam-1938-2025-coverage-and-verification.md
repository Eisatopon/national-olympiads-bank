# Putnam coverage and verification

The public collection `undergraduate/Putnam-problems.json` covers 1965–2025 with 732 records. The 240 records from 1965–1984 have source-reviewed English statements and LaTeX, primary thematic categories, source references and matching statement-check snapshots. Four original diagrams are retained as three diagram-only assets. Three printed source issues are separately noted. The 492 records from 1985–2025 retain their previous source-review status; twelve received rendering-only repairs for nested dollar math inside text macros; this import does not certify them retrospectively.

The 1938–1964 Scholes archive has been recovered for all 25 held editions, with 331 numbered statements. Both 1958 competitions are distinct; no competitions are invented for 1943–1945. All 331 identities match the existing early research candidates. The Scholes author explicitly reworded the statements, so this is a secondary-source comparison corpus. Numeric token differences are a triage aid, not proven mathematical errors. The early archive is not yet source-certified or published.

The discrepancy between 331 numbered records and the book description of 347 problems still needs a consistent counting rule for alternative questions and multipart questions; it does not establish 16 missing numbered records.

The project owner reported oral permission for publication on 2026-10-12 and authorized incremental publication of reviewed Putnam batches. Only statements and necessary diagrams are imported; no solutions are included.
