# Balkan MO — source check (8 Oct 2026)

The Balkan MO has no single official archive. Two sources were used.

- **2026, official.** The "Official Problems and Solutions" of the 43rd BMO (Thessaloniki), from the Hellenic Mathematical Society's site: https://hms.gr/43bmo2026/problems.html.
  - The four stored statements were replaced by the official wording.
  - The only real difference was P1. The AoPS text named the sets "Aristotelian"; the official text does not.
- **2005, 2007–2013, 2015–2020, 2022, 2023: UK team leader reports.** These are the UK reports on imo-register.org.uk (`YYYY-balkan-report.pdf`), and each one reproduces the contest paper.
  - They are a reliable printed copy, but not the official sheet. The check therefore confirms mathematical agreement, not exact wording.
  - All 64 statements agree mathematically with the stored text. No corrections were needed.
  - Where the PDF text layer lost formulas (2008, 2015), the page image was checked.
  - Small wording differences were kept. For example, 2016 P4 says "1 × 1201 or 1201 × 1", which is symmetric and equivalent.

Source checks were recorded for these 68 problems. Each one names the source, the page and the document's SHA-256, and states which kind of source it is.

## Not checked
The remaining years are AoPS-only and unverified:
- 1984–2004;
- 2006, 2014, 2021, 2024, 2025. There was no UK report for these years, and the 2024 and 2025 student reports do not contain the paper.

## Junior Balkan MO
Official English papers were found on the host websites for five years:
- 2013: jbmo2013.tubitak.gov.tr
- 2023: jbmo2023.al
- 2024: jbmo2024.tubitak.gov.tr
- 2025: jbmo2025.1c.mk
- 2026: jbmo2026.ssmr.ro

All 20 stored statements agree with the papers. The formulas were checked on the page images. Source checks were recorded for these 20 problems.

The 2021 paper (jbmo2021.ance.gov.md/problems.pdf) is listed in the Wayback Machine but could not be downloaded. The other years remain AoPS-only and unverified.
