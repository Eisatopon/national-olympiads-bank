# Uruguay senior final retrieval follow-up — 10 October 2026

## Result

No new statements added. The bank still holds all 28 statements of the seven Level V finals 2011–2017. The 2018–2025 gaps remain open. Current whole-bank inventory is 20,250 statements; the grid and application are unchanged.

This follow-up tested actual downloads, rather than treating indexed PDF text as recovered examination pages. In particular, the 2020 and 2022 indexed statements cannot yet be imported under the project's visual source-review requirement.
