# USA AMC tab — import log (15 Oct 2026)

New tab **USA AMC** (scope `amc`, continent group Americas). Problems © Mathematical Association of America; the heading subtitle of every row says so.

## Policy (owner decision 15 Oct 2026)
- Text: public copies of the AoPS wiki (HuggingFace datasets: CreativeMath, minyichen, edev2000, sudhanshu), SECONDARY source, no source badges. Where every wiki copy lacks the text, LIVE (Po-Shen Loh) text is used and recorded per problem. No hints or solutions.
- Figures: drawn by the bank in Asymptote — from the wiki's [asy] code (AoPS helper modules replaced by our own shim) or redrawn to match the original; never copied from LIVE.
- Every statement is cross-checked against a second copy (LIVE); wording differences recorded in the per-contest ledger.
- One row per contest; each paper (A/B, I/II, Fall, P) is a 'day' with paper_label and is numbered from 1 (validate_data.py PER_PAPER_NUMBERING). Literal dollar signs are written \$ inside math.

## AIME (key `aime`) — 1983–2026 complete, 1,065 problems
