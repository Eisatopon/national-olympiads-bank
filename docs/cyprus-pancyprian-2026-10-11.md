# Cyprus senior national archive — 11 October 2026

Added **78 statements in 19 complete Grade 12 Pancyprian examinations**, in 18 calendar-year cells: 2006, 2007, 2009–2019 and 2021–2025. The competition is the proof-based CMS Pancyprian Mathematical Competition, not the separate multiple-choice Cyprus Mathematical Olympiad. Existing Cyprus senior TST data remains 184 statements, in 46 stored examinations across 18 years.

## Dates and source evidence

All 17 official CMS archive pages were retrieved, including the older-years page. All 19 corresponding Grade 12 documents were downloaded; only Grade 12 statements were taken from combined Grade 10–12 documents. Actual rendered PDF pages, not search snippets or text extraction alone, were inspected for every imported statement. The exact source URL, document SHA-256 and reviewed page numbers are in `cyprus-pancyprian-sources-2026-10-11.json`; per-statement mapping and fingerprints are in collection metadata. Three source diagram crops are stored, including figures printed alongside solutions; no solution text is stored. Greek original vertex labels remain in these crops: C=Γ, D=Δ, L=Λ, S=Σ, with other named Greek symbols retained in the English text.

The archive uses school-year labels. The 2020–21 paper was actually held **28 February 2021**; the 2021–22 paper was held **18 December 2021**. These occupy day 1/day 2 of calendar 2021, not fictitious 2020 and 2022 entries. The 2025–26 paper was held **13 December 2025**, so is stored in 2025. The old 2006 publication labels itself December 2006–07; the exact day remains unestablished.

75 statements have original-source comparison records. Three have visible source qualifications instead of exact-source badges:

- **2015 P4:** only four sequence terms and an ellipsis are printed; no unique continuation is specified. The printed question is retained with an explicit warning.
- **2016 P2:** the paper prints a strict inequality, false at a=b=1. The displayed inequality is changed to ≥, with the correction disclosed.
- **2018 P3:** the statement omits AB<CD, explicitly required in the official accompanying solution. That condition is added and disclosed.

## Remaining country gaps and retrieval outcomes

- Grade 12 before 2006, and the 2008 examination, remain unretrieved. AoPS has existing 2004/2005 pages, but their retrieval returned HTTP 403; 2008 is a red-link placeholder, not an available examination. The complete Grade 12 official archive retrieved here is exhausted, not the entire country's historical coverage.
- No separate calendar-2020 Pancyprian paper is established: its school-season paper was held in February 2021. Do not report 2020 as a proven held examination missing from the bank.
- The official senior TST archive still supplies only 2025 test A as its newest examination. Tests B/C/D of 2025 have official announcements but their full papers were not retrieved. 2026 senior test announcements, including D on 5 May, do not contain an exam paper.
- Within stored TST seasons: 2011/2012 lack C; 2014–2019 lack D; 2020–2022 and 2024 lack C/D; 2023 lacks D; 2025 lacks B/C/D. Before 2009, apart from 2005 A/B, full senior papers remain unretrieved. These are absent labels, not a blanket assertion that every test was held in every historical year.
- AoPS senior TST year/day catalogues include red-link placeholders (tested 2004/1, 2005/3, 2006/1 and 2011/3); a catalogue label is not evidence of an available complete paper.
- The mathematica.gr archive index `https://www.mathematica.gr/forum/viewtopic.php?t=33886` was located, but actual access hit its browser challenge; the mail subdomain also failed. Do not claim its attached material was reviewed.
- The separate CMS multiple-choice Olympiad and younger grade papers are not substituted for missing senior final/TST examinations.
