## Source errors and editorial handling

Three Croatia records do not receive exact-source badges:
- 1993 P4(b): source prints m ≥ 3, contradicted by the equilateral triangle, whose cotangent sum is √3. The bank explicitly corrects the bound to m ≥ √3 and discloses the change.
- 1995 P2: source only requires a ≠ c and b ≠ d, allowing reversed pairs such as 5 = 1²+2² = 2²+1². The bank explicitly requires distinct unordered pairs {a,b} ≠ {c,d}; the correction is disclosed.
- 1996 P2: source uses n without specifying its range. The bank explicitly clarifies n as a positive integer and discloses this assumption.
