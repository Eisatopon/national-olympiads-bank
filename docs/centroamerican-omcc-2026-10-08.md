# Mathematical Olympiad of Central America and the Caribbean (OMCC), 1999–2023 (8 Oct 2026)

New regional row: key `omcc`, file `Centroamerican-omcc-problems.json`, continent group *americas*.
- 143 problems from 24 years: 1999–2006 and 2008–2023.
- Day 1 is P1–P3 and day 2 is P4–P6.

## Sources
Every statement is a faithful English translation of an official Spanish text:

| Years | Source |
|---|---|
| 1999–2002 | Official OMCC pages of the OEI, archived (`oei.es/oim/icentro.htm` … `ivcentro.htm`). The formula images were read from the GIFs. |
| 2003–2006 | Official papers hosted by the OEI, archived: `CentroD1/D2.pdf`, `dia1/2.pdf`, `viomcc.pdf`, `centro/Prueba.pdf`. Each was checked against the rendered page image. |
| 2008–2023 | *Revista Tzaloa* of the Olimpiada Mexicana de Matemáticas: the issue that reports each edition. Several issues interleave the statements with the solutions; only the statements were taken. |

A `statement_checks` record was written for all 143 statements. Each record holds the source URL, the page, the document SHA-256 and the figure hashes.

## Gaps
- **2007** (IX OMCC): no official text was found online.
- **2024–2026**: Tzaloa issues for these years carry no OMCC section, and no other official source was reachable.
- **2017 P1**: the problem refers to a hexagonal mesh shown in a figure that Tzaloa 3/2017 does not print. It is omitted rather than guessed, and is recorded in `KNOWN_GAPS` and in `missing_problem_numbers`.

## Notes on dates
- The XXII OMCC (2020) was held online in October 2020.
- The XXIII OMCC (2021) was held online in August 2021.
