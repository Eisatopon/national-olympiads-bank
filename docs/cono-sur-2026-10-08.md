# Cono Sur Mathematical Olympiad, 1988–2026 (8 Oct 2026)

New regional row: key `cono`, file `Cono-sur-problems.json`, continent group *americas*. It holds 204 problems from 34 years: 1988, 1992–2006, 2008–2014 and 2016–2026. Each year has six problems. From 1996 on, day 1 is P1–P3 and day 2 is P4–P6. The papers from 1988 to 1995 give a single list with no days.

## Source
Every statement is a faithful English translation of the official Portuguese paper published by the Olimpíada Brasileira de Matemática (obm.org.br, *Provas e gabaritos* → Cone Sul).

The text layers of the older papers drop most mathematical symbols (≠, ≤, exponents, fractions, the integer-part brackets). Every formula was therefore read from the rendered page image, at 300 dpi where needed.

A `statement_checks` record was written for all 204 statements. Each record holds the source URL, the page, the document SHA-256 and the figure hashes.

## Missing years
- 1989 and 1990: the OBM does not publish these papers.
- 1991: the OBM link (`conesul91.doc`) returns "page not found".
- 2007 and 2015: the OBM page lists no paper for these years.

## Notes on individual statements
- 1999 P6: the official figure shows a turn with an angle labelled 80°, but the caption says "turn of 60° to the right". It contradicts the text, so it is not reproduced. The statement is complete without it.
- 2009 P5: the paper prints "k ∈ A (k = 1001)", which would make the operation trivial. It is rendered as k ≤ 1001, the condition needed to select k of the 1001 numbers.
- 1994 P2: condition (i) is translated literally ("the angle P₀AB is less than 1"), because the paper gives no unit.
- 2008 P1: the paper uses the ceiling ⌈n/2⌉, confirmed on the page image.
