- 1967: an outside correspondent reported that his reference for 1967 differs from ours. The stored four statements (from AoPS) agree with the official report in every condition; they are kept and now carry source checks.
- 1970 and 1980: the reports state a two-day final but not which problem was set on which day; the printed order (P1–3 / P4–6) is used.
- 1959 P1: the report prints a hint ("It can be solved by completing to a parallelogram"), omitted (statements only; recorded as a note).
- 1959 P3: the statement refers to the drawing of the axial section; the report's Obr. 19 is included as `images/cs_1959_p3_fig.png` (note: its auxiliary labels may belong to the solution).
- 1970 P2: source ambiguity about how the right angles at B and C are distributed (note).
- 1968: printed point values omitted.

Totals: 20 statements added; 24 source checks; 3 notes. Czech-Slovak final now 1952–2026 continuous (category A).
