## Source route and date convention

The full public [Weizmann archive](https://www.weizmann.ac.il/math/gillis/%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%93%D7%9E%D7%95%D7%AA) was fetched, its table parsed, and all 41 linked problem PDFs retrieved, including the 1972–1982 book. Missing links in the table are not proof that a contest was not held.

Modern labels retain the Hebrew school-year ending year already used by the collection: 2017 = exam 21 December 2016; 2019 = 29 November 2018; 2020 = 28 November 2019; 2023 = 14 December 2022; 2026 = 23 October 2025. This is disclosed rather than treating the 2026 label as a future exam. The historical PDF linked under 5755 is headed **1994**, so it was added as 1994; it is not used as evidence for a 1995 set.

The historical PDFs 1990–1998 contain chapter headings and book page numbers: they are an institutional published compilation, not original exam sheets. Their 58 statements receive no original-paper badges. The editorially corrected 2017 P4 also receives no exact-source badge. The other newly added 100 statements were visually and textually compared with Weizmann's official papers/publications. This describes source fidelity, not independent mathematical solution verification.

## Disclosed clarifications

- 2017 P4: exclude `(x,p)=(0,-1)`, an actual exception to the printed rational parametrisation. This is an explicit editorial correction without an exact-source badge.
- 1994 P5: require the vector being normalised to be nonzero; the historical text omits this domain condition.
- 1990 P6: require `n >= 3` when demanding that n points are not all collinear.
- 1993 P4: the source itself says the proposed inequality is false and requests a counterexample. That instruction is retained; no guessed geometric correction.
