# Czech-Slovak MO 2000–2026: checked against official sources (14 Oct 2026)

All 162 stored final-round category A statements of 2000–2026 (from AoPS) were compared, page image by page image, with official sources:
- Slovak MO annual reports "Správa o riešení úloh" (skmo.sk ids 2168…2154) for 2000–2014, and single-year Slovak national-round sheets (skmo.sk ids 1366, 1871, 2314, 2725, 3119, 3447, 3575, 4049, 4439, 4922, 5394, 5863) for 2015–2026;
- the official English booklets of the Czech MO ("NNth Czech and Slovak Mathematical Olympiad", matematickaolympiada.cz) for 2007–2016, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2023.

Result: 82 kept unchanged; 80 replaced — almost all by the official English booklet wording (same mathematics as the stored text), plus 2006 P4 ("construct" the set of centroids) and formatting/figure fixes. No mathematical errors were found in the stored 2000–2026 texts; the official English booklets themselves are defective in 2013 P2, P6, 2015 P3, P6, 2022 P3 (missing hypotheses or garbled names) — there the faithful translation of the Slovak original is kept and checked against the Slovak sheet; 2011 P1 has an editorial correction of the booklet's mistranslation ("congruent", not "the same" circumcircles); 2010 P5 restores a sentence the booklet omits (from the Slovak statement).

2008: the Czech and Slovak finals used different papers (the Slovak final had to be moved and got a completely new set of six problems; Slovak report id=2160, p. 6). The stored 2008 problems are the Slovak final (days 1–2); the Czech final of 9–12 March 2008 is now added from the official English booklet as days 3–4 (P7–P12), with a visible note on all twelve.

From 2022 the Slovak sheets call the two exam days "1. / 2. časť celoštátneho kola" — the same six-problem final (results list six scores, max 42).

Figures re-cropped from the official papers: cs_2015_p2_fig.png, cs_2023_p6_fig.png, cs_2024_p3_fig.png (the older czsk_* crops stay in the repository for the historical topic audit).
