## Policy
The bank stores the **international KSF paper** (Kangourou sans Frontières), not a national variant. National organisers sometimes replace problems, change names, numbers or choices. Reference text per year:
- 2005–2023: the English paper of the Pakistan organiser (IKMC, headed "KSF <year>"), except where it is itself a national variant (e.g. Student 2018 swaps 7 problems; 2016 Q29; 2022 Q1): there the KSF problem is taken from the version that the other national papers (Austria, Cyprus, Canada, Saudi) share, and the record's source points to that paper.
- 2024–2025: question order, numbers and choices from the Saudi Mawhiba English booklet; wording and personal names from the KSF text as given by Cyprus/Austria/Sweden (Saudi localises names).
- 2026: the Cyprus Thales Foundation paper (no other complete English copy yet), cross-checked with Sweden and Austria.
Figures are cropped at 200 dpi from the papers (Cyprus artwork used where it is the same drawing and the reference is a poor scan); Greek answer letters relabelled A–E. Picture answer choices are inside the figure ("Choices (A)–(E) are shown in the figure."). Statements only; no answers.
