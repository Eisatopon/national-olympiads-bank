# Ireland IrMO: checked against the IrMO Compendium (8 Oct 2026)

**Source:** the *Irish Mathematical Olympiad Compendium*, a 90-page PDF dated Oct 2026: https://maths.ucd.ie/~dukes/Irish_Maths_Olympiad_Compendium.pdf. It contains the papers of 1988–2026 and was compiled at UCD by the IrMO organisers.

## Method
- The compendium was split by year and paper. In 2000 and 2004 Paper 2 restarts its numbering at 1, and the split handles this.
- All 395 stored statements were compared with the compendium text, by words and by digits.
- Every mismatch was checked by hand. Most of them were page numbers or formulas lost from the text layer.
- Every problem that refers to a figure was checked on the page image.

## Result
No mathematical errors were found, and the text already followed the papers.

Changes:
- **Official figures added**, together with the official wording that refers to each one ("as shown", "as in the diagram", "as shown below for the case …"):
  - 1989 P1
  - 2001 P7 (three hoops)
  - 2006 P6 (5 × 6 building)
  - 2010 P4 (coin triangle)
  - 2012 P10 (mice board)
  - 2017 P4 (triangular grid)
  - 2020 P4 (2-level honeycomb)
- **2005 P8:** the official note defining "towers" recursively replaces the paraphrased note.

All 395 statements now have source checks.
