# UK BMO1 / BMO2 — check against the official UKMT papers (8 Oct 2026)

Source: the official past papers at https://bmos.ukmt.org.uk/home/bmo.shtml (`bmo1-YYYY.pdf`, `bmo2-YYYY.pdf`). The file year is the year of the summer term, so `bmo1-2005.pdf` is the Round 1 paper of 1 December 2004. This matches the bank's year labels.

Method: each paper was split into questions and compared with the stored text by words and digits. Then every BMO1 2005–2019 page was compared visually with the new text. A source check was recorded for all 220 statements: 132 for BMO1 and 88 for BMO2.

## BMO1 (`uk1`)
- **2005–2019 (89 statements).** The stored texts were paraphrases, not the UKMT wording; for example, 2010 P6 dropped the Long John Silverman story. All of them were replaced by the official text, including the official notes, for example:
  - "Note that 0 is an even number";
  - the definition of an altitude in 2012 P6;
  - the $[XYZ]$ notation in 2014 P5 and 2017 P5.

  No mathematical error was found in the old paraphrases.
- **2020–2026.** These already matched the official papers. No changes were needed. The bracketed "[Section A — answer only]" tags in 2021 are editorial and were kept.

## BMO2 (`uk2`)
All 88 statements already followed the official wording. The official notes that had been dropped were restored:
- 2007 P3: definitions of circumcentre and orthocentre;
- 2009 P2: circumcentre and orthocentre;
- 2010 P2: centroid;
- 2012 P2 and 2017 P2: the examples of $\lfloor x\rfloor$.

None of these papers has a figure.

Possible extension: the same UKMT archive has the BMO papers for 1973–1991 and BMO1/BMO2 for 1992–2004. These are not yet in the bank.
