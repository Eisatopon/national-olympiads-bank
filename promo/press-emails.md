## 3. News outlets and agencies (press release)

**Subject:** PRESS RELEASE – Greek educator launches free global bank of 19,400+ math olympiad problems

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Greek educator launches free global bank of 19,400+ math olympiad problems**

*Athens, [date]:* eisatopon.gr today announces the **World Mathematical Olympiads Bank**, a free online collection of more than 19,400 mathematical olympiad problems from 68 countries, covering 1947 to 2026.

The bank brings together national olympiads, team selection tests, regional competitions and every International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) since 1959. All problems are presented in English with mathematical typesetting, with their sources recorded and searchable by topic. Teachers and students can create printable problem sets in one click.

"Olympiad problems are a treasure of mathematical creativity, but they were scattered across the world in many languages," said Sokratis Romanidis, creator of eisatopon.gr. "Our goal is to make them freely available to every student and teacher, wherever they are."

The bank is free, needs no registration and is updated regularly with new competitions.

## Tips
- **Send Tuesday to Thursday, 9–11 am in the recipient's time zone.** That is when emails are most likely to be read.
- **Follow up once.** If you get no reply after 7–10 days, send one short reminder ("Just following up on my note below…").
- **Use the Greek version for Greek press.** Translate press release 3 (ΑΠΕ-ΜΠΕ, Καθημερινή, esos.gr, alfavita.gr). Tell me if you want me to write it.
- **Do not attach large files.** Use the link to the image instead.
