eisatopon.gr presents the World Mathematical Olympiads Bank, the largest free collection of mathematical olympiad problem statements. Every problem is in English, with properly typeset formulas.

What's inside

  • National olympiads and IMO team selection tests from 68 countries: Greece, Russia, China, the USA, Romania, Bulgaria, Poland and many more.
  • Regional competitions: Balkan MO, JBMO, APMO, Baltic Way, MEMO, Benelux, Nordic, Iberoamerican, Cono Sur, Centroamerican.
  • International competitions: every IMO since 1959 and EGMO, checked against the official texts.

What you can do

  • 🔎 Search by keyword or by topic: Algebra, Geometry, Number Theory, Combinatorics.
  • 🖨️ Build and print a problem set in one click.
  • 🔗 Share a collection of problems with your students via a link.

Ideal for students preparing for national olympiads, the Balkan MO or the IMO, and for teachers looking for material for math circles and olympiad training.
