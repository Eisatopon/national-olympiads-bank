What you may do

Pythagoras is free to use for personal study and for teaching: read the problems, print problem sets for your own students, export single problems to LaTeX for your own work, and share links to the site.

What needs written permission

Without written permission from eisatopon.gr you may not: download the collection in bulk or with automated tools (scraping, crawling, mirroring); republish a substantial part of the bank on another website, app, book or database; build a derivative problem bank from it; or use its content to train or evaluate artificial-intelligence systems.

What is protected

The transcriptions in LaTeX, the translations, the redrawn and cropped figures, the selection, classification and organisation of the problems, the software and design of the site, and the database as a whole (the sui generis database right of EU Directive 96/9/EC and Greek law 2121/1993, articles 45A ff.). The problems themselves remain the work and property of their authors and organising committees; Pythagoras reproduces them for study, with the source of every problem.

Competition organisers may ask for corrections or removal of their problems, and anyone may ask for permission to reuse content, through eisatopon.gr.
